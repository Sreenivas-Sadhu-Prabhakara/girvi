# BIS IS 1417:2016 — gold fineness grades (extract)

Captured 2026-07-23 from Bureau of Indian Standards materials
(https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/brief-on-Hallmarking.pdf and
the BIS hallmarking FAQ).

> "IS 1417:2016 permits hallmarking of six caratage (fineness in ppt) of gold
> jewellery/artefacts, viz. 14K(585), 18K(750), 20K(833), 22K(916), 23K(958)
> and 24KS(995)."

girvi's purity picker is exactly this list; 916 (22K) is the reference purity
against which the typed rate is proportionately applied (RBI Para 17 fallback).
