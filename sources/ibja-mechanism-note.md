# IBJA daily rates page — mechanism snapshot (no rate shipped)

Observed 2026-07-23 at https://ibjarates.com (page showed rates dated 2026-07-22).

The India Bullion and Jewellers Association publishes daily opening (AM) and
closing (PM) gold rates at purities **999, 995, 916 (22K), 750 and 585** (per
10 g, excluding GST and making charges) and silver 999 — the published
benchmark that RBI Para 17's "lower of the 30-day average and the previous
day's closing price" rule points at.

girvi deliberately ships **no rate value** from this page and never fetches one
(the CSP forbids all network access). This note documents only that the
mechanism exists as the Directions describe it.
